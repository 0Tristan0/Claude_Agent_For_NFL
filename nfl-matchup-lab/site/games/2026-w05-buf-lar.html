The short version

Biggest mismatch: Rams passing game

The Rams throw for the 2nd most yards in the league and face a Bills pass D that ranks 29th in yards and TDs allowed. This is where the game tilts.

Bills path: run it and let Allen move

The Rams are #1 against the pass but closer to average against the run. Buffalo gets 5.05 yards a carry (3rd) and Allen has 7 rushing TDs.

Swing factor: turnovers

Stafford has 6 INTs (tied 29th), but Buffalo barely forces picks (25th). The Bills gave the ball away 7 times in their last two games.
