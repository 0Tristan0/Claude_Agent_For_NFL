How the play is made

Each standard PrizePicks line gets an estimate the way the original Bills-Rams report did it: (recent + matchup + market) / 2, where recent is his last 4 games at the line pulled toward 50%, matchup is this game's matchups, and market is 50% when the board has no price. The best line per player (3+ games, 52.5% or better) is a pick; entries are the top picks from this game only, both teams. The Guesser then splits $20 across up to 3 entries (2 to 6 picks, Power or Flex) to grow a pretend bankroll fastest, which puts most of it on the entry most likely to pay. Chances multiply the picks' estimates as if independent.
