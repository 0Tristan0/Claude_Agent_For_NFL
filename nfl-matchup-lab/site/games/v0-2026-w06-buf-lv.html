How much to trust this

Checked on every 2024 regular-season game from week 3 (240 games, 16,278 graded props), with the model fitted on 2021 to 2023 only. Lines in that check are stand-ins (each player's recent median), not PrizePicks lines, so real hit rates will be lower. 2024 was also used to choose some v0 settings, so this is somewhat optimistic; the untouched test is the 2025 season.
