How much to trust this

Checked on every 2024 regular-season game from week 3 (240 games, 16,278 graded props), with the model fitted on 2021 to 2023 only. Lines in that check are stand-ins (each player's recent median), not PrizePicks lines, so real hit rates will be lower. 2024 was also used to choose some v0 settings, so this is somewhat optimistic; the untouched test is the 2025 season. Brier score 0.182 vs 0.201 for a simple baseline (his last-8-game hit rate pulled toward 50%, no market; lower is better). When the model said X%, here is how often it happened:

How the projections are built: targets and carries come from each player's recent share of team volume (shrunk toward what his snap share predicts), team volume from the market total and spread, and efficiency shrunk toward position averages. Each game is then simulated 20,000 times.

Model v0: a simplified first version (team-level simulation, fixed shrinkage strengths). Data: nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, rosters, injuries and schedules (CC-BY 4.0); stadium time zones from the greerreNFL/Stadiums dataset; PrizePicks payouts from its help center. Market lines are the latest in the nflverse schedule when this page was built. Actives: players with a snap in the team's last 2 games, minus Out/Doubtful; add returning players in data/overrides.csv. These are estimates with real uncertainty, not betting advice.
