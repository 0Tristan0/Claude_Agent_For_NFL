Your lines

From data/props.csv. Model chances are calibrated on 2023 (raw sim chances were too confident at the extremes). When the model is very far from a real line (72% or more on one side), the line probably reflects news the model doesn't have, so those legs never qualify. Break-even per leg: 3-pick 58.5%, 4-pick 56.2%, 5-pick 54.3%, 6-pick 54.2%. A leg qualifies only if the model beats that and the no-vig market price by 3 points.

How much to trust this

Checked on every 2024 regular-season game from week 3 (240 games, 16,278 graded props), with the model fitted on 2021 to 2023 only. Lines in that check are stand-ins (each player's recent median), not PrizePicks lines, so real hit rates will be lower. 2024 was also used to choose some v0 settings, so this is somewhat optimistic; the untouched test is the 2025 season.
