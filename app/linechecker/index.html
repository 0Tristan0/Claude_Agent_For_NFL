How to read this. Each percentage is the model's chance that the stat finishes on that side of the line, assuming the player plays (apps usually void a pick if he does not). The range shows how much that chance moves when his own game history is resampled. Combined chances multiply the picks as if they were independent; picks from the same game are not, so treat those numbers as rough.

Limits. The model has never been tested against pick'em lines, which are set by sharp markets. In its 2025 held-out test it showed no demonstrated advantage over a simple workload baseline for 100+ yard outcomes. Quarterback changes, injuries and weather are not inputs.
